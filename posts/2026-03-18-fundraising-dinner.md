This afternoon and evening, local Huntington restaurant Christopher's Eats has graciously allowed offered to donate 10% of all dining proceeds to Malawi Med. This money will help send 16 local medical students as they travel to Ekwendeni, Malawi in April of this year.

The doors open at 11am -- come and join us for a night of good food and learning about the Malawi Med vision!
