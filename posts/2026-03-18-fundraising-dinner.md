This afternoon and evening, local Huntington restaurant Christopher's Eats is supporting the upcoming Malawi Med trip by donating 10% of all dining proceeds. This money will help send 16 Huntington medical students as they travel to Ekwendeni, Malawi in April of this year and support the incredible work of local Ekwendeni physicians to provide health care in the Warm Heart of Africa.

The doors open at 11am -- come and join us for a night of good food and learning about the Malawi Med vision!
