## Tips for writing posts

Use `##` for section headings, `###` for sub-headings.

To add a photo with a caption:

![Your caption goes here](posts/photos/your-photo.jpg)

To add a photo with no caption, leave the alt text empty:

![](posts/photos/your-photo.jpg)

Photos go in the `posts/photos/` folder. Upload them to GitHub alongside the `.md` file. Both landscape and portrait photos are handled automatically — portrait photos are capped in height so they don't dominate the page.

To add a header photo, fill in the `header_photo:` line at the top of the file with the path, e.g. `header_photo: posts/photos/your-header.jpg`. It will display as a full-width hero image behind the post title.

**Bold text** and *italic text* work as expected.

> Blockquotes like this are great for memorable moments or quotes from patients or colleagues.
