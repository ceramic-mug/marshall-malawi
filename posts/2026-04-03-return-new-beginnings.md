I am so honored and excited to come back to a corner of the world that is as rich in its culture as it is its hospitality. I look forward to meeting with physicians and local leaders to discuss how they plan and prepare for community issues, and how we may apply their strategies to rural Appalachia. I believe everyone will have the opportunity to learn from their culture, build new relationships, and have a strong understanding of the potential impacts of global health trips like this. 

As we were leaving home, our crew was met with a series of travel related obstacles that continued even through US airports. Despite this, not only were we able to stay with our original travel plan, but I can proudly say each member of our team acted with resilience, cooperation, composure, and optimism. 

I am so grateful that I am able to return with a strong, ambitious team of students, and I feel with much confidence that this will be another amazing experience. 

Thomas Mosman
Fourth-Year Medical Student
Future Family Medicine Physician
From Wheeling, West Virginia
