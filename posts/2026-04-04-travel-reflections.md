Two car rides, 3 flights, and 39 hours later, we made it all the way from Huntington to Malawi! As someone who has not traveled outside the United States (aside from the Caribbean), this was definitely a unique first experience. Traveling as a group of 16 means a few trials and tribulations were expected. Although some of my peers had trouble with their plane tickets, they never let it affect their experience. As a group, we were able to remain positive and hopeful and came up with multiple alternatives for each possible scenario. I am so grateful to be traveling with a group that consistently ensured everyone was safe and remained together. 

I also experienced my first overnight flight on this trip, which was actually pretty cool! Seeing the sky go from day to night so quickly as we traveled forward into eastern time zones was really interesting. We were served dinner, a late night snack, and breakfast. Once we landed in Lilongwe, Malawi, we began the last stretch to Ekwendeni. I was instantly enthralled by the beauty of this country and the kindness of the Malawians. We drove by many villages and markets on our way, and each time we would pass children, their excitement was contagious. They would give us the biggest smile and wave. Every single group we passed, it was such a heartwarming welcome. It was instantly obvious why Malawi is referred to as the “warm heart of Africa”. These encounters were definitely my favorite part of the travel experience. 

After traveling 7 hours of bumpy roads, we finally reached our destination. I was exhausted, hungry, and in desperate need of a shower, but seeing the excitement of those hosting us upon our arrival immediately changed my mood. We arrived pretty late in the night, but they were still elated to greet us, show us to our rooms, and get us settled in. They also prepared a delicious warm meal that was ready for us after we settled in. I am still in awe of the hospitality they continue to show. The warm welcome of everyone here has greatly eased my anxiety about being so far away from home for the first time. We have already made so many fun memories that I will look back on for years, and I am so excited to see how much I will learn and for all the other memories that will be made over the next 3 weeks.

Courtney Lulek
Second-Year Medical Student
MD/PhD Student
From Russell Springs, KY
