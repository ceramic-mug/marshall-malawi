In 2020, the Malawian Government published a comprehensive proposal of the country's economic goals for 2063 and a roadmap to meet those goals. This document is "Malawi's Vision 2063" for "An Inclusively Wealthy and Self-reliant Nation." 

Malawi as a country faces many of the same challenges as Appalachia -- a history of exploitation and governance by outside forces, lack of internal capital, delayed technological adoption, and dependency on aid. Malawi's Vision 2063 document provides an example for us Appalachians on how to name, measure, and solve our economic issues.

I'm writing this blog to share a link to Malawi's Vision 2063 and condense the "Pillars" and "Enablers" of the Vision in text here for your review. I think a cursory pass through the components of the Vision will provide the introspective reader with meaningful ideas about our own home place -- the stimulation of Appalachian economy, strengthening of political integrity and self-governance, investment in our youth, strengthening of agriculture and manufacturing, and willpower as a people.

## Malawi Vision 2063

Link to the Vision: https://npc.mw/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/MW2063-VISION-FINAL.pdf

Summary:
*“We envision a youth-centric inclusive wealth creating and self-reliant nation by 2063” (Introduction).*

### Pillars:
1. Agricultural Productivity and Commercialization
    1. Agricultural Productivity
    2. Agricultural Commercialization
    3. Improved and Sustainable Land Management Practices
    4. Optimal Utilization of Land Resources
    5. Climate Smart and Resilient Agriculture
    6. Diversification of the Agriculture Sector
    7. Increased Use of Modern Technologies and Farm Mechanization
    8. Access to Targeted Agriculture Insurance
    9. Development of an Agricultural Finance Policy
2. Industrialization
    1. Industrializing Mining
    2. Increased Access to Affordable Finance
    3. Increased Transport and Storage Infrastructure
    4. Investment in Power Generation and Access
    5. Diversification and Value Addition in Export Products and Competitiveness
    6. Promotion of Research, Science, Technology and Innovation
3. Urbanization
    1. Tourism
    2. Regulated and Controlled Infrastructure Development Planning
    3. Creation of Secondary Cities
    4. Domestic Revenue Mobilization
    5. Enhancing Own Source Revenue Capacities

### Enablers:
1. Mindset Change
    1. Visionary and Transformative Leadership
    2. Patriotism
    3. Religious Values
    4. Integrity
    5. Hard Work and Self Reliance
    6. Building Positivity
2. Effective Governance Systems and Institutions
    1. Citizen Engagement and Participation and Fair Conduct of Elections
    2. Responsiveness
    3. Efficiency and Effectiveness
    4. Openness and Transparency
    5. Rule of Law
    6. Ethical Conduct
    7. Competency and Capacity
    8. Innovation and Openness to Change
    9. Sustainability and Long-Term Orientation
    10. Sound Financial and Economic Management
    11. Human Rights, Cultural Diversity, and Social Cohesion
    12. Accountability
    13. Sustainable Peace and Security
3. Enhanced Public Sector Performance
4. Private Sector Dynamism
5. Human Capital Development
    1. Education and Skills Development
    2. Science, Technology and Innovation
    3. Health and Nutrition
    4. Managing Population Growth
    5. Clean Water, Sanitation and Hygiene
    6. Gender
    7. Sports
6. Economic Infrastructure
    1. Energy
    2. Transport
    3. Information and Communication Technology
    4. Irrigation Infrastructure
7. Environmental Sustainability
