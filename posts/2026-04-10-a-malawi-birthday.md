In Malawi, birthdays are celebrated in a way that highlights just how meaningful simple things can be. They are celebrated not through gifts or decorations, but through providing something meaningful to others. 

It’s common for someone to mark their day by buying rice, beans, and a chicken, not just as ingredients, but as symbols of a special occasion. For many families, these items are a luxury, and are reserved for special moments. Birthday celebrations are also accompanied by music, dancing, and of course, a cake. Their traditions are a reminder that what may seem simple elsewhere can carry deep significance, and that celebration is really about appreciation, community, and sharing what you have. 

Today is my birthday. I was showered with joy and singing from the start, walking into the dinning area with balloons and singing. I rotated on maternity in hopes to be a part of bringing new life into the world, delivering babies who had the same birthday as me. Unfortunately, that did not happen but I still gained so much value information. 

Upon leaving the hospital, we started our journey to the Mushroom Farm where we faced a rough and uneven terrain. After four long but comical hours, we arrived. We had dinner that was unexpectedly followed by a Malawi staple, cake.

My birthday wasn’t anything like I imagined, but in the best way. It was filled with unexpected moments, genuine connection, and heartfelt joy.

Mckensie Spencer
Third Year Medical Student
From Lucasville, Ohio
