Joy in Malawi is not hard to find. Around every corner you find a wave, a thumbs up and a smile. The children especially are so excited to greet us, jumping up and down over two thumb up, and I can see their smiles even if it’s with a distant wave. When the distance closes, high fives spread cheer like a wild fire. 

Joy in Malawi is laughter and dancing in a circle with a local band where even the bartender joins in. When you’re pulled into the middle by a friend or a stranger, you can’t help but laugh and smile as you try to find the beat of the drums. Joy is found jumping fully clothed into the pool of a waterfall during a hike in the mountains of Livingstonia where one brave person rippled into a group of 16 encouraging each other to experience the natural beauty of Africa. 

Joy is easy to experience here. From the simplicity of a wave to the mountains of Africa, people smile through it all and that is a direct depiction of the true joy of the people of Malawi shared and welcomed to any visitors.

Olivia McCoy
Fourth Year Medical Student
From Charleston, West Virginia
