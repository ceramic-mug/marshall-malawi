When embarking on this journey to Malawi, I thought I had an idea of what I was going to experience in rural Africa, but nothing could have quite prepared me for this amazing and eye opening experience. I was instantly in awe of the sheer hospitality and kindness the people here have shown our group. When Malawi is referred to as the “Warm Heart of Africa,” it is immediately evident through everyone here. 

Not only are the people so altruistic, but have an admirable ability to express their love for their heritage and country through art and dance. Over this past weekend, our group made a trip to Livingstonia, and stayed at The Mushroom Farm. On Saturday night, a group of 6 young musicians and dancers came and performed traditional Malawian song and dance. It was absolutely wonderful to see them expressing themselves through their talents of music. The songs sang words of their pride to be African, and their dance was beautifully rhythmic and on best with the instruments. Much of their performance displayed a type of creativity that I have never personally seen with my own eyes. It was truly wonderful to see and participate in. 

My short time in Malawi thus far has not only taught me gratitude, compassion, and humility, but has also given me great friendships and has educated me so much in another culture that I have never experienced before. I look forward to the last 8 days we have here, and to be reminded of what an exceptional opportunity this experiences has been in every aspect of my life.
Holly Edwards
Fourth Year Medical Student
Incoming Anesthesiology PGY1
From Hurricane, WV
